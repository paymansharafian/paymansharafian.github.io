Designed and implemented a neural human intent estimator that infers operator intent in real time from teleoperation inputs. The estimator uses projected gradient descent (PGD) to compute intent confidence, enabling smooth adaptive blending between operator control and autonomous safety behaviors on ARNA.

Presented at IEEE CASE 2024.
