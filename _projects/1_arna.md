ARNA (Adaptive Nursing Assistant Robot) is an NSF-funded custom omnidirectional mobile manipulator developed at LARRI for clinical nursing assistance. The platform integrates a Kinova Gen3 arm on a Mecanum-wheel base with LiDAR, ATI Axia 80 F/T sensors, and a full ROS1 software stack.

My role spanned the full system lifecycle: hardware debugging and upgrade, sensor calibration, driver development, and supporting clinical deployment studies involving 58+ nursing students and 15+ hospital patients.
