Designed and fabricated a sensorized handlebar for ARNA from scratch: SolidWorks CAD, FDM 3D printing, and integration of 8-channel FSR tactile arrays with ATI F/T force/torque sensing.

Engineered a multimodal perception pipeline fusing 16-channel tactile data with 6-axis F/T signals through a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) trained with Bayesian-optimized focal loss, achieving 85% detection accuracy at 2.05ms mean latency. Paper under journal review.
