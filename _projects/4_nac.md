Developed and fine-tuned a model-free NAC architecture for admittance-based pHRI on ARNA. The controller uses MLP neural networks to approximate nonlinear robot dynamics online, enabling real-time adaptation to changing interaction forces and patient behavior.

Validated stability using Lyapunov analysis and demonstrated reduced tracking error and motion jerk compared to baseline PID and conventional admittance controllers in hardware experiments.
